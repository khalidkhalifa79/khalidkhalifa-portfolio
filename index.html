Business Management System / Admin Panel / Certificate Management Platform

Safety Horizon System

Safety Horizon System is a custom-developed internal business platform built to manage certificate workflows, client records, product information, and administrative operations within a structured database-driven environment.

The system was designed to support multiple certificate categories, controlled user access, organized data handling, and final print-ready PDF generation with stamp, signature, and QR code integration.

  • Secure login and role-based access
  • Multi-type certificate management
  • Client, company, and product record handling
  • Producer, importer, exporter, and office management
  • Certificate preview and final printable PDF output
  • Structured business workflow support inside the admin interface
