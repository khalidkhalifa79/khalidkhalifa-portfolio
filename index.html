Business Management System / Admin Panel / Certificate Management Platform

Safety Horizon System

Safety Horizon System is a custom-developed business management platform built to handle certificate workflows, client records, product information, and internal administrative operations within a secure, database-driven environment.

The system supports multiple certificate categories, structured data management, controlled user access, and operational efficiency through a dedicated admin interface. It also includes certificate preview and final print output functionality, allowing users to review certificate content before generating the official printable PDF version with stamp, company signature, and QR code integration.

  • Secure login system
  • Multi-type certificate management
  • Admin dashboard and record control
  • Client and company management
  • Product, scope, and standard management
  • Office, producer, importer, and exporter handling
  • Role-based access and permissions
  • Certificate preview for content review
  • Final print-ready PDF generation with stamp, signature, and QR code
